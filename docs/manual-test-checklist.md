# Manual test checklist

## Pointer input

- Tap the button at a steady pace at least eight times.
- Confirm that a BPM value appears after the second tap.
- Change pace and confirm that the value follows the recent taps.
- Pause for more than 2.5 seconds and confirm that the next tap starts a new count.

## Keyboard input

- Focus the page and press Space at a steady pace.
- Confirm that Space does not scroll the page while it records taps.
- Hold Space and confirm that key repeat does not create extra taps.

## Layout and accessibility

- Test at a 320 CSS pixel viewport without horizontal scrolling.
- Confirm that the Tap and Reset buttons remain reachable.
- Confirm that the result is announced through the live region.
